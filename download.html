(03) Setting up

Watch it once, then follow along.

Video · Coming soon
  1. 1

    Open the .dmg

    Drag Laysidaysi into your Applications folder. Until Apple has notarized it, run this once in Terminal, then open the app:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Laysidaysi.app
  2. 2

    Say hello

    A short welcome walks you through it and you make your first profile together.

  3. 3

    Add the browser add-on

    Install it from Firefox Add-ons or the Chrome Web Store. The app shows when it's connected.

  4. 4

    Allow it in private windows

    In Firefox, turn on “Run in Private Windows” in the add-on's settings. In Chrome, turn on “Allow in Incognito” under Details. Then strict blocks cover those too.

  5. 5

    Plan your first block

    Double-click on the timeline, or press “Start focusing now”. That's it.
