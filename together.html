(01) What helps you focus

Small habits,
big blocks.

A few tricks to start with, from us. Try one in your next session, then tell us what works for you.

Phone

Put your phone in another room before a block. Out of sight really is out of mind.

Planning

Give your hardest task the first block of the day, before you open email.

Profiles

Only a few sites pull you away? A Block only profile stops them and leaves the rest open.

Breaks

Plan your breaks and step away from the screen. You'll come back rested, not scrolled out.

Planning

Before a block ends, write one line: where you stopped and what comes next.

Strictness

Start gentle and turn it up slowly. Strict on day one can feel like too much.
